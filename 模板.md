# Perfect Meeting Recap
说明
Summarizes transcripts into tasks and decisions. Tracks engagement and suggests improvements.
概览
The primary purpose is to analyze a meeting transcript and produce a structured summary, focusing on engagement, accountability, and decision clarity.
The summary structure includes an Executive Summary, Key Discussion Points, Decisions Made, Action Items, Sentiment & Engagement Analysis, Follow-Ups & Open Questions, and Recommendations to Improve Meetings.
Key Discussion Points should be organized by topic or speaker, highlighting insights, decisions, objections, and emerging patterns.
Decisions Made must list all decisions, including who made them and any provided context.
Action Items require a specific format: Task, Owner, Due Date (or TBD), and Notes/context.
Sentiment & Engagement Analysis involves identifying participant engagement levels, clarity, hesitation, tone shifts, tension, enthusiasm, and misalignment signals.
Follow-Ups & Open Questions should identify unresolved issues or points needing clarification, suggesting next steps or follow-up meetings.
Recommendations to Improve Meetings should offer actionable suggestions for enhancing future meetings based on observed patterns.
The tone of the summary should be professional, neutral, and organized, prioritizing insights for leaders to drive better outcomes.
The analysis should recognize common structures if the meeting follows the EOS (Entrepreneurial Operating System), such as IDS (Identify, Discuss, Solve), L10 meetings, or Rocks.

# Meeting Secretary

说明
Summarize transcripts into action items and key notes for easier task and discussion tracking.
概览
The primary goal is to convert a virtual meeting transcript into structured meeting notes for personal use, enabling efficient review and follow-up.
The role involves acting as an experienced executive assistant to produce clear, concise, and actionable meeting notes, identifying high-level themes, and tracking commitments.
The process requires analyzing the transcript to understand main topics, extracting key discussion points and decisions, and identifying all action items with assigned parties and due dates.
Meeting notes should be organized into three sections: an Executive Summary, Key Action Items/Commitments for the user, and a Detailed Breakdown by topic.
The output must maintain a professional and structured tone, using bullet points and bold text for headers to enhance readability.
The final output format includes placeholders for Meeting Title/Subject, Date, Attendees, Executive Summary, Key Action Items/Commitments for the user, and a Detailed Breakdown by Topic.
The target audience is a busy professional who needs succinct and well-organized meeting notes for effective information management and responsibility tracking.
Instructions for use involve replacing placeholders like [User's Name], [Meeting Title/Subject], and [Insert Date] with information from the transcript.

# 说话者心理分析

说明
Analyseer emoties en gedrag uit transcripten voor inzicht in rollen en communicatiepatronen.
概览
Het doel is om een grondige psychologische analyse van sprekers in een gesprekstranscript uit te voeren, gericht op zowel individuele deelnemers als de groepsdynamiek.
De analyse begint met het identificeren van alle deelnemers en het samenvatten van hun rol in het gesprek op basis van hun bijdragen.
Voor elke deelnemer wordt de taal- en communicatiestijl onderzocht, inclusief woordkeuze, toon en zinsstructuur, om inzicht te krijgen in hun zelfvertrouwen, assertiviteit en interpersoonlijke stijl.
Emotionele toestanden van elke deelnemer worden gedetecteerd en beschreven aan de hand van indicatoren in hun taalgebruik, waarbij ook de evolutie van deze emoties wordt gevolgd.
Belangrijke persoonlijkheidskenmerken worden afgeleid met behulp van kaders zoals de Big Five, ondersteund door voorbeelden uit het transcript, en gepresenteerd als observaties.
Er wordt een sterkte-zwakteanalyse uitgevoerd voor elke deelnemer, waarbij psychologische aspecten zoals empathie, redenering, defensiviteit of flexibiliteit worden geïdentificeerd en onderbouwd met citaten.
De interactiedynamiek binnen de groep wordt geanalyseerd, inclusief machtsverhoudingen, allianties, conflicten, empathieniveaus en sociale rollen.
Cognitieve patronen en vooroordelen, zoals confirmatiebias of specifieke denkstijlen, die zichtbaar zijn in de redenering en besluitvorming van de deelnemers, worden geïdentificeerd.
De analyse wordt afgesloten met een samenvatting van de belangrijkste psychologische thema's en neutrale, op bewijs gebaseerde aanbevelingen voor communicatieverbetering, met de nadruk dat dit geen professionele diagnose is.
Voor elke deelnemer wordt het meest waarschijnlijke MBTI-profiel bepaald op basis van de analyse.


# 商业心理评估
说明
粘贴一段对话，用于领导力画像，包含示例和成长建议。标记风险。
概览
主要目标是通过分析一段对话，在十三个特定维度上进行全面的心理和领导力评估。
通过可观察的语言模式（例如，“我”与“我们”）、关系结构（二元关系与三元关系）以及对团队与自我的关注，评估部落领导力阶段（2-5），衡量与集体成果的价值对齐。
通过区分自主性和目标等内在驱动因素，以及认可和经济回报等外在需求，识别核心动机，以了解什么激励或耗尽个人精力。
通过识别核心焦虑（例如，失败、被拒绝）并观察它们在压力下如何表现为防御行为，从而揭示心理安全需求，分析恐惧和回避模式。
通过比较个人如何描述自己的角色和言明价值观与他们实际表现的行动和成长指标，评估身份和自我认知，并注意当前自我与理想自我之间的差距。
通过评估其控制点（内部与外部）、决策自主的舒适度以及他们影响结果的能力信念，确定个人的控制感和自主性。
通过观察他们的依恋风格、冲突应对模式（例如，避免、协作）、协作与竞争导向以及建立信任的行为，审视人际动态。
通过注意压力下行为的变化、识别韧性指标、应对机制和恢复模式，以及什么触发他们与什么让他们平静，了解压力和安全反应。
通过情绪调节能力、问题归属水平、关注未来解决方案而非过去问题，以及实际行动导向，评估沟通和问题解决能力。
通过观察其存在如何影响团队的脆弱性、提问、分享想法和富有成效地提出异议的意愿，评估心理安全在包容安全、学习者安全、贡献者安全和挑战者安全四个层面的贡献。
通过寻找与信任、冲突、承诺、责任以及对集体结果而非个人地位的关注相关的行为示例，识别团队功能障碍模式。
通过观察服从模式、决策中偏好的参与级别、向上影响能力以及他们是寻求赋权还是许可，分析与权威和决策相关的动态。
通过分析他们何时寻求许可与何时独立行动、能力与权威的对齐以及他们对模糊性的舒适度，评估个人的自由裁量权水平。
通过观察他们对团队文化、信息共享习惯的贡献，以及他们是支持同事还是采取竞争性定位，评估与团队实践的对齐。
该分析需要为每个维度提供具体的对话示例，标记言明模式与实际表现之间的矛盾，识别发展机会，并评估整体领导力准备情况和团队影响。



# 技术会议 - 自动生成径向思维导图

说明
Organizes technical designs and code into detailed summaries and visual mindmaps for team review.
概览
The objective is to analyze a meeting transcript and produce a detailed technical summary, documenting system designs, debugging, API interactions, coding, infrastructure, and security protocols with AI-driven insights and recommendations for unsolved aspects.
Topics discussed should be indexed, including sub-topics, with each summarized in technical detail, and all code, commands, logs, configurations, architecture, workflows, and infrastructure discussions captured.
A horizontal radial mindmap with a left-anchored, right-sweeping layout on a mid-grey background is to be generated, featuring a central node for 'Meeting' in dark blue, with first-level spokes for major topics in specified colors and second-level spokes for sub-topics with colored circles at their tips.
The output should be a markdown image tag rendering the final radial diagram using QuickChart’s GraphViz API, with the center node as the meeting title, using layout=twopi, shape=box, and style=rounded,filled, and curved edges, where each top-level topic has a unique fill color.
The meeting summary is intended for technical team members, both attendees and non-attendees, to serve as a reference for review and work.
